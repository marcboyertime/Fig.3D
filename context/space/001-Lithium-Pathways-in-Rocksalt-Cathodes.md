## Learning progression

1. Build the relevant battery and crystal structure prerequisites.

2. Introduce one octahedral environment and one tetrahedral pathway.

3. Distinguish oxygen coordination geometry from neighboring cation positions.

4. Show local pathway classification and why local favorable channels need to connect.

5. Compare the declared layered, spinel, and disordered representations.

6. Explore composition and order only with explicit parameter meanings.

7. Return to the paper’s figure, evidence, and interpretation limits.
