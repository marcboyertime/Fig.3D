# Source-based homepage hero — current record

The native 1500×850 Figure 1 raster was extracted unchanged from page 2 of the included Hau et al. paper. The user's original screenshot and standalone HTML are preserved separately and unchanged. The HTML is a mechanism reference, not a UI template or authority for all of its numerical claims.

The 25.8-second story is a storyboard of eight named beats in hero-timeline.mjs: the whole figure, a spotlight and zoom onto the layered 1-TM glyph, the lift into 3D, oxygen coordination, the A→T→B hop in three beats (entry window, the tetrahedral site beside one transition metal, exit window) and a closing explore beat. Every caption stays up for at least max(2.8 s, characters ÷ 15 + 0.5 s), which hop-verification.mjs enforces. Layered/Spinel/DRX are comparisons, not sequential phases. Zoom does not exceed 0.95 CSS pixels per source pixel before the WebGL objects take over.

The zoom is a camera move: the rest of the page stays faintly in view and its edges soften instead of stopping at a frame. The zoomed glyph then becomes a card inside the 3D scene and lays back like a page on a desk while spheres settle onto its printed atoms and rise into the ideal geometry. A brief dolly-zoom (wider field of view, same scale on the central plane) gives the card real perspective and is gone before oxygen appears. The route sweeps in as the 3D counterpart of the printed hop arrow.

Actual Three.js physical sphere geometry and lighting replace the rejected soft Canvas gradient balls. The oxygen tetrahedron, complete endpoint coordination context, hollow vacancy/site markers, species controls and keyboard/pointer inspection follow hop-model.mjs. Edges and faces depth-test against the atoms, each oxygen window lights as lithium crosses it, and the one face-sharing transition metal tints its face while lithium waits at the tetrahedral site. A second gate vacancy remains present. See hop-scientific-audit.md for science and assumptions.

Headline connectors are single smooth arcs: a hairline that strengthens toward its target, a small origin dot and a narrow notched head. The one from "figures" points at the figure's visible edge and slides to the glyph as the spotlight lands; the one from "understanding" points at the model and both fade before lithium moves. Stacked layouts (phones, portrait tablets) underline the word instead of drawing a long arrow. Labels are placed greedily so they never overlap each other or leave the stage, and keep their side while it still works. Captions cross-fade. The model becomes explorable just before oxygen appears, so it can be inspected during the hop. The homepage battery feature is a live label-free purpose-composed render of the actual cell model, not a module screenshot or generated generic cutaway. It has short entry/hover/focus motion and a reduced-motion static state.

Source/model notes describe ideal geometry, symbolic size and illustrative timing. No migration energy, rate, performance or percolation threshold is calculated. Human expert review is optional. See the parent ENGINEERING_STATE.md and evidence directory for actual verified scope and remaining work.
