A smaller distance.

The diagram uses the analytical solution for diffusion into a sphere. The surface concentration is held constant; concentration starts at zero throughout the interior. Diffusivity is constant and the sphere does not change shape.

The concentration profile is shown at the same illustrative elapsed time and diffusivity for each radius. The characteristic diffusion time scales as R² / D. Doubling the radius gives four times the characteristic time at fixed D.

c(x, τ) = 1 − 2/(πx) Σ [ (−1)ⁿ⁺¹ sin(nπx) / n ] e−n²π²τ

Here x = r/R and τ = Dt/R². The preview uses D = 10⁻¹⁶ m²/s, t = 2 s, and 40 series terms. These are illustrative inputs, not measured properties of a particular cathode.

The rotatable 3D view uses the same concentration field, sampled on the sphere’s surface and two cutaway planes. Its movement rotates the view; the elapsed diffusion time stays fixed. The cutaway is a viewing aid, not a change to the sphere’s boundary conditions. Displayed particle sizes are schematic.

The model omits particle interactions, electrode reactions, changing diffusivity, phase transitions, and electrochemical boundary conditions. It cannot predict a real battery’s capacity or charging speed.
